How to install

iPhone / iPad: open this site in Safari, tap Share → Add to Home Screen, enable Open as Web App if shown, then Add.

Android: open in Chrome, then choose Install app or Add to Home screen from the menu.

Open the installed app and check your records. If they appear empty, connect Cloud sync and load your cloud copy, or import a backup. Keep a backup before switching.

Never enter full card numbers, PINs, or CVVs. Verify benefit eligibility and resale terms with your bank.
